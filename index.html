Selected independent research

Working mathematics, not just visual concepts

These independent research projects demonstrate techniques transferable to geometry, mathematical verification and scientific computing. Explore the live models yourself. They are not previous client commissions, engineering approvals or proof of a broader metaphysical interpretation.

Case study 01

Certified planar geometry

Independent reproduction and analysis of the Śrī Yantra constraint system, including precise geometric intersections, verified constraints and a research atlas of 128 distinct certified plane configurations. The display exports precomputed vector geometry.

Transferable skill: bringing mathematically complex patterns into a reproducible geometric specification.
Case study 02

Geometry across curved surfaces

Computational reconstruction of the Śrī Yantra on spherical caps using great-circle geometry, independent construction checks and a 3D atlas. The published research atlas records 968 certified roots of the specified constraint subsets; these are mathematical research results, not physical prototypes.

Explore live spherical atlas ↗ · Viewer source · Archived release
Transferable skill: precise geodesic and constrained designs for curved-surface visualisation.
Case study 03

Geodesic and Goldberg modelling

Python research implementations for parameterised icosahedral geodesic meshes, Goldberg polyhedra and subdivision of convex polyhedra. These supply a basis for investigating non-standard dome and sculptural configurations; build-ready fabrication details require a separate project-specific development and validation stage.

Transferable skill: programmatic vertices, faces, subdivision families and mathematical shape variations. Selected results can be shared without disclosing private research code.
Case study 04

E8 projections and 3D scientific viewers

Research software for high-dimensional lattice projections, rotation searches and interactive spatial visualisation, including a public 3D globe with compare modes and reproducibility checks. The scientific interpretation of planetary alignments is separate from these transferable computational tools.

Transferable skill: precise geometric networks and interactive, data-driven spatial tools.

About

Research-led. Evidence-first.

I am an independent researcher with a background in fundamental physics and space science. My work combines mathematical reasoning, Python scientific computing, geometric reconstruction, numerical testing and interactive software.

I collaborate remotely with artists, designers, specialist fabricators, engineering teams and independent researchers whose ideas call for careful mathematical or scientific analysis.

An investigation assesses evidence rather than endorsing a predetermined interpretation. Some metaphysical claims are not empirically testable. Structural engineering approval, building compliance and safety certification remain the responsibility of appropriately qualified professionals.

“Preserve the idea. Make its mathematics precise enough to test, and its results clear enough to use.”

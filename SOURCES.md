# Visual provenance and claims

All illustrations on this website are derived from Salah-Eddin Gherbi's existing geometric research. There is no stock illustration, generative-AI art, or claim of a physical prototype.

## `assets/sri-yantra-plane.svg`

Copied **verbatim** from the author's published plane atlas figure `bundle/figures/000.svg` in `salahealer9/sri-yantra-constraint-viewer` (main branch, consulted 2026-10-12).

Source: https://github.com/salahealer9/sri-yantra-constraint-viewer/blob/main/bundle/figures/000.svg

This is **one certified plane census root**, not the separate Rao plane-optimum reference figure. The atlas contains 128 distinct certified plane configurations. The viewer's research record distinguishes certified roots from any tolerance-dependent figure equivalence counts.

## `assets/sri-yantra-spherical.svg`

New **orthographic drawing of existing, previously certified coordinate data**, based on the actual three-dimensional great-circle polylines stored in the author's spherical viewer bundle for stable ID:

`S_1-2-3-10-16-20__root_0__481265b5`

Source: https://github.com/salahealer9/sri-yantra-spherical-viewer/blob/main/bundle/figures/S_1-2-3-10-16-20__root_0__481265b5.json

This root is recorded as Gate-4 valid, drawable, human-checked and `canonical-looking`. The visual is a sampled orthographic representation of the repository's polyline coordinates, **not a physical dome design** and not a new scientific result. The original atlas retains the certification and full coordinate data; the image does not independently recertify any construction. Visual-form classifications are editorial, not mathematical certification labels.

## `assets/geodesic-mesh.svg`

Regenerated **class-I frequency-4** icosahedral geodesic mesh, using the author's existing mesh-construction formulas and topology from the private `salahealer9/earth-grid-geodesic-analysis` repository:

- `tri_subdivide.py` – integer barycentric grid, spherical normalisation, triangular faces.
- `goldberg_utils.py` – normalised icosahedron, class-I subdivision and global vertex de-duplication.

The geometric model has **162 vertices, 320 faces** and is rendered orthographically. The image was generated with a separate internal rendering script, retained outside this public site package to avoid distributing source derived from the private research repository. The illustration is a mathematical sphere mesh, **not a physical building or a fabrication-ready construction**. Private research source code is not included.
